# Clubhouse Announcement Notes

Date: 2026-06-07

Purpose: short verbal announcement for a notary community meeting.

## Short Version

I put up a public route-first notary briefing in the Notary Geek public knowledge repo. It is not sales copy. It is a practical checklist for notaries: how to route a transaction before trusting a platform, trainer, recipient phone comment, or AI answer.

The core point is simple: do not pick the platform first. Identify the document type, the public-official signature, the notary state, the transaction date, the identity method, the output format, the destination, and the actual recipient evidence. Then decide whether the platform fits.

## Useful Framing

This is for notaries who want to avoid being used by bad guidance, platform marketing, or training shortcuts. It covers:

- new private documents versus existing public records;
- why a state named inside a document does not automatically choose the apostille state;
- Hague apostille finality versus outside-apostille authentication/legalization;
- paper originals versus remote/electronic notarial output;
- why a phone comment is not a real rejection;
- why platform, NNA, trainer, and market comfort are not safe harbor;
- why "no criminal intent" does not make bad training good guidance.
