# New York apostille and certificate of authentication: agent route guide

**Reviewed September 29, 2026 UTC · Partial New York route pilot.** [Structured record](us-ny-apostille-authentication.json) · [existing detailed birth-certificate issuer route](../../topics/new-york-birth-certificate-apostille-route.json) · [coverage map](../coverage.json)

Start with the issuer and the **Public-Official Signature Route**, not the words “New York State” printed on the paper. New York Department of State authenticates qualifying New York public documents after any required County Clerk step. The [existing Notary Geek birth-certificate record](../../topics/new-york-birth-certificate-apostille-route.json) explains the three birth issuers in more detail; this guide connects them to other document categories and current submission procedure. [NY DOS instructions](https://dos.ny.gov/apostille-certificate-authentication)

## Issuer and document branches

| Presented record | Route before New York Department of State |
| --- | --- |
| **NYSDOH-issued** birth, death, or marriage certificate | If actually issued by NYS Department of Health and signed by the Director of Vital Statistics or NYS Registrar, NY DOS says **no County Clerk certification** is required. “New York State” text or a local “registrar” title alone does not prove NYSDOH issuance. |
| **NYC Health** birth/death certificate | Obtain the **long form plus Letter of Exemplification**, have the pair authenticated by the **New York County Clerk**, then submit to NY DOS. This applies to NYC Health records across the five boroughs; do not choose a clerk solely from borough of birth. |
| Other **local or county** birth, death, marriage, or divorce certificate | Have the **County Clerk of the county where the record was issued** certify it before NY DOS. Inspect the issuing office and qualifying signature first. |
| Educational record | An institution official certifies it as an official record or true copy, that official's signature is notarized, then the notary's signature is certified by the County Clerk where the notary is qualified. |
| **NYS DCJS** background check | Include the document and the cover letter signed by the DCJS representative. NY DOS says that signature does **not** require notarization. A federal FBI check goes to the federal route. |
| Private power of attorney or similar notarized document | Obtain the proper New York notarization and County Clerk certification where the notary is qualified before NY DOS. This generic notarized-document sequence does not replace the issuer-specific vital record rules. |

The [NY DOS page](https://dos.ny.gov/apostille-certificate-authentication) and [official request form](https://dos.ny.gov/apostillecertificate-authentication-request-forms) support these branches. NYC Health's [birth-certificate page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/doh/services/birth-death-records-birth.page) describes its long-form/Letter sequence.

## Current NY DOS submission

The state lists **$10 per document** with its current request form naming the destination country. It returns completed documents by first-class mail, or express service with a prepaid label; it does not accept labels that bill a sender or credit card. USPS mail goes to **PO Box 22001, Albany, NY 12201-2001**; express carriers use the physical **99 Washington Avenue, 6th Floor, Albany, NY 12231** address. The [live NY DOS page](https://dos.ny.gov/apostille-certificate-authentication) says walk-in same-day service has resumed at NYC, Albany, Binghamton, Buffalo, and Utica, capped at **10 documents per customer**. NYC/Albany drop-offs are processed by receipt date, without counter priority.

**Address discrepancy:** NYC Health's embedded apostille Step 3 still prints a **19th-floor** NY DOS NYC address and older hours. The [current NY DOS apostille page](https://dos.ny.gov/apostille-certificate-authentication) and [form](https://dos.ny.gov/apostillecertificate-authentication-request-forms) list **123 William Street, 2nd Floor**. Use the live DOS location and check again before visiting.

The NY DOS page warns that credit card requests need the newest form with a **CVV**. The fetched PDF displays DOS-1917-f (Rev. 02/24) and a CVV field; confirm its current download before sending payment. An incomplete form is returned. The prior NYSDOH vital-record page in the repo returned a 403 during this review, so this guide makes no new claim about that office's ordering or forwarding process.

## Destination and remaining depth

New York issues an **apostille** for Convention destinations and a **certificate of authentication** for non-Convention destinations, which may need further U.S. Department of State handling. Confirm current [HCCH status](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41) and the receiving institution's documentary requirements. **Recipient Evidence** is distinct from state certification; a birth record apostille does not prove the birth facts to a foreign registry.

This pilot extends the earlier birth-specific work and identifies school, DCJS, and private notarial branches. Business certificates, court records, exact electronic output acceptance, and destination-specific requirements need further primary-source review. This is public guidance, not acceptance of a particular request or a deadline promise.
