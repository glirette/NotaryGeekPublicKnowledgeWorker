# Texas universal apostille: agent route guide

**Reviewed September 29, 2026 UTC · Partial Texas route pilot.** [Structured record](us-tx-apostille-authentication.json) · [coverage map](../coverage.json) · [Notary Geek routing model](https://notary.cx/notarial-routing-model.json)

The Texas Secretary of State issues a **universal Apostille** as its state certificate for records used abroad. For a destination outside the Apostille Convention, Texas says further U.S. Department of State authentication is needed. The same state certificate label does not make the outside-Convention chain disappear. Follow the **Public-Official Signature Route** and actual destination. [Texas overview](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/authinfo.shtml) · [FAQ](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/authfaqs.shtml)

## Document branches

| Presented document | Texas route |
| --- | --- |
| State or county **recordable** public record | Obtain the original Texas-issued or official certified copy, not a notary-certified copy of a recorded item. Texas says certified records must be issued within **five years** for apostille. Examples include birth/death, court and county records. Identify the issuing official and seal. |
| **Non-recordable** private document | The issuer or signer supplies a typed/written statement explaining contents and intent, signs it, and receives a proper Texas notarial certificate with seal, notary signature, and date. List every attachment and include the full set. A notary cannot assert the signer's legal capacity or make the signer's statement. |
| Identity-document copy or translation | For an identity document copy, Texas requires the original owner/signature route. A translation needs the translator's signed, notarized statement and the original or copy of what was translated. |
| Texas **RON** document | The Secretary of State explicitly says online notarization documents **can** be apostilled with a ledger showing signature date/time, document description, and signer information plus a certificate identifying electronic/online two-way audiovisual notarization. The receiving country or institution may still reject RON. Confirm the exact output package and recipient policy. |
| SOS-filed company certificate or certified business filing | Contact the **Corporation Certifying Team** for an original certified formation document or certificate of fact. Texas says the ordinary Authentications unit does not handle these original SOS business records. A private business copy with an authorized company statement and Texas notarization is a different signature route. |
| Federal or other-state record | Texas will not authenticate federal or another state's original public record. Select the authority for the official signature instead of the requester's address. |

Sources: [Texas apostille page](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/authinfo.shtml), [agency guide](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/statdoc/guides/apostilleguide.pdf), and [mail request instructions](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/statdoc/request.shtml). Review the existing [Texas law source pointers](../../law/notary-law-source-index.json) for underlying notarial authority questions.

## Current submission choices

| Lane | Published route |
| --- | --- |
| Mail | Original Texas issued/certified record or original notarized document, [Form 2102](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/statdoc/request.shtml) with destination country, and **$15 per document**, paid as instructed. Texas estimates up to **two weeks from office receipt** and will not pull a mailed request into walk-in service. |
| Austin counter | **400 West 15th Street**. Walk-ins Monday/Friday; appointments Tuesday–Thursday. Same-day lane has a **10-document limit per individual, company, or transaction**. Bring eligible records and payment; the page accepts check, money order, card, or exact-change cash at the office. |
| Bulk drop box | More than ten documents: register through the office's QR drop box process. The page describes processing within **72 hours**, with pickup or prepaid, labeled return shipping; verify current rules before a deadline. |
| Adoption | Use **Form 2103**, $10 per document and $100 maximum for each child's adoption, subject to the same document eligibility review. |

These are volatile office and processing statements from the [current request page](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/statdoc/request.shtml), not delivery or outcome promises. The office has posted a business filing portal transition for September 29, 2026, so recheck the company-record ordering path and counter notices.

## Two separate acceptance decisions

Texas agency acceptance of a properly packaged RON document is an **agency output** fact. Whether a foreign recipient accepts an electronic/online notarial act is **Recipient Evidence**; the [Texas page](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/authinfo.shtml) warns that some countries do not. Likewise, its universal state certificate may need [federal authentication](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/replace-certify-docs/authenticate-your-document/authentication-certificate-requirements.html) and potentially destination steps when the Convention path does not apply. Check [HCCH status](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41) and the actual recipient.
