# Florida apostille and notarial certification: agent route guide

**Reviewed September 29, 2026 UTC · Partial Florida route pilot.** [Structured record](us-fl-apostille-authentication.json) · [coverage map](../coverage.json) · [Notary Geek routing model](https://notary.cx/notarial-routing-model.json)

The Florida Secretary of State is the state's designated competent authority, and its Division of Corporations issues apostilles and outside-Convention notarial certifications in Tallahassee. First follow the **Public-Official Signature Route** on the actual record. An apostille confirms the issuing official's or notary's signature; it does not prove the document's contents or the recipient's acceptance. [Florida overview](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/) · [FAQ](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/faq/)

## Choose the document branch

| Presented document | Agency-specific route |
| --- | --- |
| Birth or death certificate | The [accepted-document guidance](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/accepted-documents/) calls for a Florida Bureau of Vital Statistics record signed by the State Registrar, or a County Health Department record **signed by the State Registrar**. Pre-2004 birth records may contain limited information; check the receiving consulate's requirements. Issuing office fees are separate. |
| Marriage or dissolution certificate | Use the Bureau of Vital Statistics certificate signed by the State Registrar or the Clerk of County Courts as specified. Identify the signer and any separate procurement fee. |
| Public school or university transcript | The school registrar or issuing official signs the transcript and it is notarized with a full notarial statement. Do not treat a later personal printout and notarization as equivalent to the official issuing path. |
| Private school or university transcript | The page describes a **printed transcript attested as a copy**, signed by the student or custodian, with a full notarial statement. This is a distinct branch from a public school transcript. |
| Florida Secretary of State company certificate or certified copy | Submit the **original issued** certificate/copy, or request it with the apostille. The latter uses **two separate fees and two separate checks/money orders**: one for the official certificate/copy, one for the $10 apostille. |
| County Clerk of the Court certified record | The [main page](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/) lists **$20 per document** when seeking an apostille on a Florida county Clerk-certified document, comprising $10 for the apostille and $10 for a Certificate of Incumbency. |
| Private document notarized by a Florida notary | The original must carry a complete notarial statement in English and the notary's original signature, stamp, and seal. The agency says Florida notaries cannot attest copies of listed public records, including birth/death/marriage certificates and court-filed records. Follow the official copy's own issuer route. |
| Federal document or another state's official/notarial signature | The Florida page directs these to the federal or other state's competent authority. A Florida requester or courier does not change the public official whose signature is authenticated. |

These branches come from the [accepted document categories](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/accepted-documents/) and [request page](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/). Use the existing [Florida Chapter 117 section corpus](../../law/us-fl/florida-chapter-117-2025.json) to investigate a specific notarial act or copy rule, while checking the current statutory year before relying on it.

## Submit and keep the output lane intact

Florida requests its [apostille/notarial certificate form](https://dos.fl.gov/media/702388/1-apostille_and_notarial_certificate_request_form-10-19-2023.pdf), an original certified copy or certificate of status for a public document, or an original notarized document with full act. It rejects photocopies. Enclose a self-addressed stamped envelope or prepaid pre-addressed air bill with the requester named as **both sender and recipient**, plus a check or money order payable to Florida Department of State. Cash and credit cards are not accepted. The office is **2415 N. Monroe Street, Suite 810, Tallahassee, FL 32303**. Check current form and address before mailing. [Official instructions](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/)

The standard stated fee is **$10 per document**; the Clerk-certified branch above is **$20 per document**. The office says it has **no expedited service**, but allows walk-ins in Tallahassee and says most walk-in requests may be completed while one waits. It processes requests in received order. A private courier cannot promise agency priority. [FAQ](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/faq/)

The [processing dates page](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/document-processing-dates/), updated **September 28, 2026**, showed apostille requests received **September 2** and certification requests received **September 21** as currently being processed. Those are separate, dated queues; neither is an end-to-end turnaround estimate.

**Remote/Electronic Output Lane:** The cited agency pages require an original notarial signature, stamp, seal, and full statement, and reject photocopies. They do not settle how a particular remote online notarization electronic original or printout would be accepted for apostille. Get a current output-specific answer from the issuing office before making that claim. A provider's ability to complete a Florida RON session is a different fact.

## Destination and recipient evidence

Check the current [HCCH Convention status](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41) for apostille versus Florida's notarial certification outside the Convention. The [Florida FAQ](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/other-services/apostille-notarial-certification/faq/) says a later U.S. Department of State authentication may be required for a Florida document, depending on the destination route. Confirm the actual consulate or receiving institution's record age, translation, certified-copy and other requirements. **Recipient Evidence** is separate from Florida issuance.

This pilot covers significant document categories and their signature/output differences. It does not assert full Florida coverage, recipient acceptance, current RON apostille eligibility, or a service deadline.
