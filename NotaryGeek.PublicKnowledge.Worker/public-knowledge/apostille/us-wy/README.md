# Wyoming apostille and authentication: agent route guide

**Reviewed September 29, 2026 UTC · Partial Wyoming route pilot.** [Structured record](us-wy-apostille-authentication.json) · [coverage map](../coverage.json) · [Notary Geek routing model](https://notary.cx/notarial-routing-model.json)

Identify the **Public-Official Signature Route** first. The Wyoming Secretary of State's [document requirements](https://sos.wyo.gov/Services/DocumentRequirements.aspx) describe original signatures of officials such as a County Clerk/deputy, State Registrar/deputy, State Examiner, or a Wyoming notary. The office uses a [Universal Certificate](https://sos.wyo.gov/services/apostille_sigauthorization.aspx) for Hague and non-Hague destinations. That state output does not by itself settle any later outside-Convention or recipient requirement.

## Company records have their own issuer lane

The [Wyoming request form, revised July 1, 2026](https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Authentication/AuthenticationForm.pdf), says business documents **must be issued and certified by the Wyoming Business Division**. It expressly says **not to send printed documents** in place of Business Division-produced certified records.

| Wyoming company item | Office route and published charge |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate of good standing or another business certificate | Obtain the Division-issued certificate; the form adds **$20** for the certificate plus **$20 per apostille**. |
| Certified articles | Obtain the official certified copy; the form adds **$10 for the first ten pages**, with more than ten pages requiring an inquiry, plus **$20 per apostille**. |
| Private operating agreement or power of attorney | The form gives these as documents to be notarized by a Wyoming notarial officer. This follows the notary's signature, a separate route from an official business filing. A Wyoming company's private document notarized in another state does not become a Wyoming-signed record merely by mentioning the entity. This is a Notary Geek routing inference. |

The form says documents requested from the Business Division **can take up to 15 business days** to process. That is not a guaranteed full apostille and international delivery timeline. Keep issuance, certification, apostille, scan/return, and receiving-party acceptance as separate events.

## Other document routes

| Document | Signature and output question |
| --- | --- |
| Vital, court, or county record | Which Wyoming official issued and signed/certified it? Obtain the official output bearing the original signature. The agency's list of example official titles is in its [document requirements](https://sos.wyo.gov/Services/DocumentRequirements.aspx). |
| Educational record | The [form](https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Authentication/AuthenticationForm.pdf) says **all educational records must be notarized by the school's Office Registrar/Notary Officer**. A student's printout notarized elsewhere has a different signature route. |
| Other private document | The form calls for a Wyoming notarial officer for documents such as operating agreements or powers of attorney. Confirm the complete original act and do not conflate a notary-signed private document with a state-issued company certificate. |
| Federal or other-state signed record | Find the designated authority for the actual signature. A Wyoming requester, registered agent, or formation state does not turn a federal or other-state record into a Wyoming public-official signature. |

**Remote/Electronic Output Lane:** The cited requirement says signatures must be original, and the company form rejects printed business documents in place of Division certified records. These sources do not establish a universal rule for all electronic notarial originals. Confirm any exact e-output with the agency and receiving party before treating a printout as eligible.

## Submission and destination

The [Secretary of State page](https://sos.wyo.gov/services/authentication.aspx) prefers mail via USPS, FedEx or UPS with the request form, **$20 per apostille or authentication**, and a prepaid return envelope/label. In-person requests are **appointment only**: call **307-777-7370** or email **Apostilles@wyo.gov**. The office lists Herschler Building East, 122 W. 25th Street, Suites 100 and 101, Cheyenne, WY 82002-0020. The [current form](https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Authentication/AuthenticationForm.pdf) says no card payments and asks for receiving country.

Check the [HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41), then the actual foreign recipient or consulate for any additional authentication, original, translation, age, or format requirement. Wyoming's **Universal Certificate** describes what the state issues; **Recipient Evidence** establishes what the destination will accept.

This pilot makes Wyoming business record issuance and separate fees explicit, as well as the school registrar and private-notary branches. It does not claim complete Wyoming coverage or accept a specific order. Recheck current form, appointment, Business Division process, fees and destination.
