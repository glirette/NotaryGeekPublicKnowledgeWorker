## Bounded merge-stage review gate

This review is reproducible: start with the adversarial input, find the guide row, match its JSON fact/branch to source IDs, open the cited agency page/form, then check the negative conclusion and open question. A later reviewer can focus on these conditions instead of relitigating every sentence:

- **Blocking for a claimed transaction route:** no identified issuing official/competent authority; a plain/printed/electronic output presented as an accepted original without agency evidence; outside-Convention chain described as complete without destination evidence; or a recipient's acceptance asserted without written recipient evidence.
- **Blocking for an unconditional current instruction:** a changed official form, fee, address, hours, portal, or queue that the guide still states as current. Refresh volatile claims at merge and again at use time; preserve dates rather than silently rolling them forward.
- **Research still outside this partial increment:** California marriage/education/court; New York company/court; exact electronic output acceptance in CA/FL/WY/NY and electronic official copy handling in DE; current NYSDOH ordering page (403 during review); destination-country legalization and transaction-specific recipient requirements.
- **Evidence status:** This is self-review of public agency pages and repository consistency. It does not include a live agency submission, recipient decision, independent reviewer sign-off, or a full 50-state content audit. Those absent facts are not encoded as passes.

A future change that resolves an open case should add dated primary evidence and update both the guide and JSON; a refusal or conflict is also useful evidence. No route gets a “complete” label merely because these adversarial cases were contained.
