Specific permitted uses exist. Ask for the permission covering that exact context. Permission alone would not establish endorsement, NIST certification or notarial-law compliance.

## KBA, revision dates and documented tailoring

[SP 800-63A-4 § 2.5.1](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63a.html), published in July 2025, prohibits KBA/KBV for identity verification. Section 3.2.1 separately permits KBV in fraud management. The [Revision 3 FAQ, Q-A4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-FAQ/) already says KBV cannot satisfy IAL2/IAL3 verification while recognizing supplemental use. This is more precise than treating all historical KBA as forbidden, or using a password/security-question rule as a substitute for the identity-proofing rule.

PCX09, CA15 and IR15 require the advertised revision, complete workflow and actual KBA role. If [tailoring](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63.html) is invoked, identify the documented deviation, compensating controls, comparability, residual risk, DIAS and required relying-party disclosure/review and risk-acceptance determination. Actual qualifying evidence can change the corresponding assessment. Hypothetical supplemental checks cannot substantiate an unknown workflow. A state-law requirement does not make KBA NIST-approved.
