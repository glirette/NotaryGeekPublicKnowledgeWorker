## The identity-method question stays concrete

The word *biometrics* does not identify the missing Virginia method. An ordinary selfie, liveness check or face comparison is not, by that label alone, the signer's qualifying digital-certificate/PIV method. The notary's certificate used to sign a completed document is a separate credential.

When KBA did not run and pass, the record must identify the actual second listed method or the separate personal-knowledge or qualifying credible-witness route. The current webpage does not disclose enough implementation evidence to answer that question for a particular NotaryCam session. That limit on this webpage's evidence does not erase Notary Geek's earlier investigation or its provider assessment.

Read this alongside the existing [Virginia research hub](https://notary.cx/virginia-ron-research-hub.html), [biometrics analysis](https://notary.cx/virginia-ron-biometrics-platform-myth.html), [identity-method history](https://notary.cx/virginia-ron-identity-methods.html) and [NotaryCam no-KBA scenario](https://notary.cx/notarycam-german-signer-no-kba-virginia.html).
