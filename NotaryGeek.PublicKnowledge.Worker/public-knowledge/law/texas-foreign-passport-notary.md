# Texas Foreign Passport Notary Source Note

Reviewed: 2026-06-10

This public note separates a narrow Texas in-person notary identity rule from broader claims about foreign passports, Mexican passports, and Texas remote online notarization.

## Short Answer

Texas has a foreign-passport lane for in-person notarization when the document relates to a residential real estate transaction. Do not treat that as a blanket rule for every Texas notarization or for Texas remote online notarization.

## Exact Texas SOS Training Language

> If notarizing a document relating to a residential real estate transaction, you may accept an unexpired passport issued by a foreign country as proof of identity.

## Issue Boundaries

- In-person residential real estate document: the Texas SOS training wording supports accepting an unexpired passport issued by a foreign country as proof of identity in this lane.
- Other in-person documents: do not assume the residential real estate wording applies. Check current Texas statute and SOS guidance before relying only on a foreign passport.
- Remote online notarization: analyze separately under Texas online notarization identity proofing and credential-analysis rules. Do not import the in-person residential real estate phrase into RON without checking the online rules and transaction record.
