# Texas Foreign Passport Notary Source Note

Reviewed: 2026-06-10

This public note separates a narrow Texas residential real estate document identity rule from broader claims about foreign passports, Mexican passports, and Texas remote online notarization procedure.

## Short Answer

Texas has a foreign-passport lane when the document relates to a residential real estate transaction. Texas SOS online training says online identification requirements are the same as traditional notarization, while online notarization adds credential analysis and identity proofing.

## Exact Texas SOS Training Language

> If notarizing a document relating to a residential real estate transaction, you may accept an unexpired passport issued by a foreign country as proof of identity.

The Texas SOS online notarization transcript also says:

> The identification requirements for online notarization are the same as for traditional notarization.

## Issue Boundaries

- In-person residential real estate document: the Texas SOS training wording supports accepting an unexpired passport issued by a foreign country as proof of identity in this lane.
- Other documents: do not assume the residential real estate wording applies. Check current Texas statute and SOS guidance before relying only on a foreign passport.
- Remote online notarization: apply the same identification baseline, then analyze the extra Texas online notarization identity proofing and credential-analysis procedures. Do not imply a different Texas passport eligibility rule for online notarization unless a current Texas source says so; online notarization adds credential analysis, identity proofing, and record requirements.
