# Virginia RON Biometric Identity Method Source Note

Reviewed: 2026-06-24

This public note separates Virginia statutory identity-method language from broad platform or market use of the phrase "biometric notary."

## Short Answer

Virginia law lists several ways identity may be confirmed for electronic notarization. The biometric language appears inside one statutory option tied to a valid digital certificate accessed by biometric data or PIV-style credentials. It should not be treated as a generic label for platform selfie, liveness, or face-match checks.

## Virginia Code Signal

Virginia Code section 47.1-2 defines "satisfactory evidence of identity" for electronic notarization and includes personal knowledge, credible witness, and combinations of listed identity methods.

The biometric-related listed option is a valid digital certificate accessed by biometric data or by use of an interoperable Personal Identity Verification card.

## Issue Boundaries

- Generic platform biometric, selfie, liveness, or face-match check: do not treat ordinary platform fraud-control features as the Virginia statutory biometric identity method unless the workflow also satisfies the specific Virginia law and current Secretary standards.
- KBA failure or no-SSN signer: a failed KBA or no-SSN fact does not automatically make a Virginia notary the right route. The answer must map the transaction-date Virginia identity method, signer evidence, platform workflow, notarial act, output, retained record, and recipient or apostille path.
- Foreign-language or foreign-passport signer: do not route by provider label such as "biometric notary." Pick the notary state and identity method that fit the signer facts before choosing a platform or sourcing a notary.
